Privacy policy

This Google Cloud project is used only by its owner to connect a private, self-hosted Home Assistant server to the owner's own Google account.

  • Google data (such as calendar events) is accessed only by the owner's Home Assistant server, on the owner's home network.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No other users are served. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page.

Contact: the project owner, through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
